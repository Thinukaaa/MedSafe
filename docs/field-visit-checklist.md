# Field visit checklist — pharmacy (tonight)

Ask permission first. Do not photograph customers, prescriptions or patient names.

## Photos to take
- [ ] Shop front (name visible, if the owner agrees)
- [ ] Medicine shelves (shows how much stock there is to watch)
- [ ] Close-up of 3–5 box labels showing **batch no. + expiry date + price**
- [ ] The expired / return box or bag (if they have one)
- [ ] How they track expiry now (notebook, stickers, nothing?)
- [ ] You + the pharmacist (stakeholder evidence), if they agree

## Questions (write the answers word for word)
1. Your name and position? How long have you worked here?
2. How do you check for expiring medicines now? How often?
3. In the last 3 months, roughly how much stock did you throw away as expired? (Rs or number of boxes)
4. Will suppliers take back near-expiry stock? **Until how many days before expiry?** Same for every supplier?
   → this sets the **supplier return window (days)** in Settings (now an ASSUMPTION of 60 days)
   → and the **warning window** (how early staff need the alert)
5. Which items expire most often? (slow-moving items, syrups, fridge items?)
6. Has anyone ever nearly sold an expired item? (safety angle)
7. Who would use an app like this — you, an assistant? On a phone or PC?
8. Would you pay a one-time Rs ___ (device + setup) for it? Rs ___ per year for support? What would make it worth it?
9. **Pharmacy margin:** on a typical tablet, what do you pay the distributor vs the MRP? (ask for 2–3 invoice lines)
   → replaces the **16 % margin ASSUMPTION** in Settings (cost = MRP × (1 − margin))
10. **Fridge:** what do you keep in the fridge (insulin, vaccines, eye drops)? Do you check its temperature? Written log?
    Ever had a power cut or the door left open?

## Fridge temperature check (5 minutes)
- [ ] Photo of the fridge (closed) and of any temperature log sheet
- [ ] If they agree: put a thermometer (or the MedSafe probe) on the middle shelf for 5 min → write °C and the time
- [ ] Note where the fridge is (near a window? on a generator/UPS during power cuts?)
- [ ] Compare with **2–8 °C** (WHO vaccine/insulin storage range)

## Data to enter into the app (real, not demo)
Enter 5–10 real batches from the shelf (scan the box barcode or search the catalogue): batch no., expiry, qty, cost price.
Cost source = **Supplier invoice** or **Pharmacist told us (field visit)**.

## For the business component (app → More → Business case)
- Monthly expired loss (from Q3 ÷ 3) → source: stakeholder interview + date
- Share prevented = **ASSUMPTION** (or press “use measured recovery rate”)
- One-time package price (from Q8 + parts receipt) → payback months = price ÷ monthly saving
- Yearly support (optional, Q8) → 3-year net benefit = 3 × yearly saving − price − 3 × support

## After the visit — update the app
- [ ] Settings: supplier return window (Q4), margin % (Q9), critical/warning windows
- [ ] Business case: monthly loss + sources
- [ ] Clear demo data before showing the real batches
